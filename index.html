The Trunk

The Trunk is a personal art studio tool made by SKALLA. It turns original artwork into short videos and saves the finished files to the owner's own Google Drive. It is used only by its owner.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026.

The Trunk signs in to the owner's own Google account only to upload the owner's own video and image files to the owner's own Google Drive. It does not collect, sell, or share Google user data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising. Sign-in credentials are stored privately on the owner's own computers.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/connections.
